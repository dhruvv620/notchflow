# Changelog

All notable changes to NotchFlow are documented in this file. Add what changes under Unreleased as you go. `Release.command` turns that section into the new version's dated section, uses it as the release notes (on GitHub and in the update window), and starts a new, empty Unreleased.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- **Orbit: your best focus hours**: from two weeks of time tracking, Orbit's Today view shows the two hours you do your deepest work (coding, writing, design, learning), like "10:00–12:00: about 1h 40m of deep work there on a typical day", and one click blocks them for today. When the block starts, notifications are held until it ends and a scene you pick can start; the morning brief mentions them too.
- **Orbit: Scattered**: jumping between 6 or more apps in 10 minutes shows "Scattered · 7 apps" under the closed notch; opening it offers Focus 25 min in one click. At most every 30 minutes, and it only listens for app switches while on.
- **Orbit: Shut down for the day**: one button in Today picks tomorrow's top 3 from your open loops (shown in the next morning's brief), saves your open windows as a workspace, backs up unsaved code in your projects to a `wip/notchflow-<date>` branch (pushed to GitHub when there's a remote; your files and current branch stay exactly as they are), holds notifications until your brief's hour, and runs Done for the day. Each step can be unticked.
- **Orbit: bills and renewals**: messages and emails about a renewal, a trial ending or a bill due become a Loop three days before the date, with the service and amount when mentioned ("Netflix renews · $15.49"). Receipts and confirmations are ignored.
- **AI Agents as a team**: the AI Agents tab opens on **All**, a board with what needs you on the left (approvals with Allow / Always / Deny, agents asking in their window, background work ready for review with See changes and Commit) and every agent at work on the right: who it is, the project, where it runs (background copy, NotchFlow's terminal, iTerm, Cursor…), the task, what it's doing right now and for how long. Tabs for **Claude**, **Codex**, **Gemini** and **Cursor** (each in its own colour, with counts and an orange dot when it needs you) show just that agent: its sessions and conversation, or its cards for agents whose chat lives in their own window.
- **Start AI tools from the notch**: Start icons at the top of the AI Agents tab start Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Cursor Agent and others in a project folder you pick (its last folder, recent projects, Orbit projects, or Choose Folder…), right in the notch's Terminal tab; hold ⌥ to open your terminal app instead. Settings > AI Agents > Command-line tools finds which are installed (only when you open it), installs missing ones in one click, chooses which get an icon, and adds your own (any command, like `ollama run llama3`).
- **Agents in the closed notch**: with several agents running, the closed notch shows one dot per agent in its colour and "N working".
- **A tab per WhatsApp account**: with two accounts linked, the WhatsApp tab shows All, then one tab per account (named as in Settings > WhatsApp, like Business and Personal) above the chat list, each with its unread count. Pick one to see only that account's chats; the choice is remembered.
- **Restart NotchFlow from anywhere**: right-click the gear in the notch for Restart (and Quit), or use Restart at the top of Settings > General. The menu bar icon's Restart now waits for the old copy to quit and reopens the same copy you were running, so two never overlap.
- **Resize any tab**: a size button in the notch's top bar (next to the gear) makes the open tab bigger: click to step through Regular, Large and Extra large, or hold for the list. Each tab remembers its own size. AI Agents and Cuely use the same sizes as their own buttons. Music and the copy card keep their fixed layout.
- **Hand-offs work in the background and come back for review**: handing a loop to Claude Code or Codex no longer opens a terminal. The agent works out of sight in its own copy of the project (a git worktree on a new branch, so your files aren't touched), with file edits allowed and commands still asking in the notch. When it's done you get "Ready for review", a Review loop, and a card in Orbit's Team view with its summary and every changed file (+/− lines): See changes opens the patch, Apply puts the changes in your project folder, Commit applies and commits just those files, Throw away deletes the copy, and Keep going sends more instructions to the same session. The project's timeline records it, and a Linear issue gets a comment. Gemini CLI and Cursor still open in a terminal. On in Settings > Orbit > Team.
- **Agents get the context with the task**: a hand-off now includes the Linear issue's description, the last failing build for that project, and the project's recent timeline, so you don't have to explain the background. Failed builds and Linear issues in Loops have a ✦ button to hand them to your default agent in one click.
- **Approve from anywhere**: when an agent asks for permission, a small card appears at the top right under the menu bar with the command and Allow / Always / Deny (and a button to open it in the notch). It never takes focus from your app and goes away once answered. Turn it off in Settings > AI Agents > Approvals.
- **Orbit (off by default)**: project and people pages that build themselves. Turn it on in Settings > Orbit. Projects come from commands you run in the Terminal tab (filed under the folder's project), AI agents finishing work, builds and focus time; people come from messages and promises. The new Orbit tab shows each project's timeline, its people, today's focus, and a note field; each person shows their projects and recent history. With "Write notes to an Obsidian vault" on, it keeps Projects, People and Daily notes in markdown with [[links]], rewriting only its own marked section so your own writing is kept. Every part has its own switch, and off means Orbit does nothing at all: no listeners, no timers, nothing in memory.
- **Orbit Loops and two-way Linear**: the Orbit tab opens on Loops, one inbox of what's still open: an AI agent waiting on you, promises (yours and theirs, overdue first), unanswered messages (one "Reply to …" per person), failing builds, and your open Linear issues. Click one to open it, tick it off, or snooze it (an hour, tomorrow, next week); a dismissed message loop comes back if that person writes again. With "Connect Linear" on and a personal API key (kept in the Keychain), Start and Done move issues in Linear, anything in Loops or a project's timeline can become a Linear issue (right-click), issue moves show on the project's timeline, and Orbit can comment on an issue when an AI agent finishes a task that names it (ENG-12). Linear is checked every five minutes only while it's on.
- **Orbit Team, Scenes and Morning brief**: Team shows your AI agents as a board (who's working, who's waiting on you, on what), and any loop can be handed to Claude Code, Codex, Gemini or Cursor from its right-click menu: the agent starts in that project's folder with the loop as its task (a Linear issue also moves to In Progress). Scenes set you up in one click: open a workspace, hold notifications for a while, open a Terminal tab in the project, and start an agent on a task; make one from your busiest project in the Today view and edit them in Settings > Orbit. The Morning brief (off by default) gives yesterday's work per project, today's meetings and what's open, as a notification at the hour you pick and at the top of the daily note; it uses one timer for that moment and nothing in between. Each has its own switch.
- **YouTube Music without opening tabs**: a song picked in the notch's search now plays without a new tab or anything coming forward. With the YouTube Music desktop app installed (with its API Server plugin on), it goes next in the app's queue and plays, the app started hidden if needed. Otherwise it plays in one YouTube Music window of your browser (Chrome Beta, Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi or Safari, signed in as you), kept just off screen and reused for every song; after 15 minutes without playing that window closes to free its memory.

### Changed
- **Choose which tab the notch opens on**: Settings > General > Opens on replaces "Remember last tab" and "Open on the tab that fits the moment" with one choice: Music, Always this tab (pick any tab), The last tab I used, or Smart. Smart now has a checkbox for each reason, so it can stop jumping to WhatsApp for unread messages while still showing Calendar before a meeting. A separate switch decides whether something that needs you (an agent waiting, a promise due) opens its tab first.
- **Orbit People is pinned-only**: People no longer fills up with everyone who messages you. Right-click a message or promise in Loops and choose "Pin to People" to give someone a page; only then are their messages and promises kept there. Unpin from their page. People added automatically before are cleared once (their old notes in the vault stay; delete the vault's Orbit/People folder if you don't want them).

### Fixed
- **WhatsApp groups show their names**: groups the gateway's chat list left unnamed showed as "Group 120363…". NotchFlow now asks each account for its groups' names (at most every 10 minutes, only when a group is missing its name) and shows those.
- **Codex conversations show in the AI Agents tab**: a Codex session (especially a new one) showed only a status card. NotchFlow now lists Codex sessions even when Codex's long opening instructions push your first message out of the part it reads, keeps looking for a few seconds while a new session writes its file, and finds a session by its folder if its id doesn't match. Agents started from a Start icon also get a Terminal button that jumps to them in the notch's Terminal tab.
- **AI Agents tab**: the approval card in the All board no longer squeezes its buttons into single letters, and an agent's tab (like Codex) opens its newest conversation, or shows its running sessions as cards, instead of an empty "Pick a session".
- **WhatsApp accounts no longer vanish from Settings**: while the WhatsApp connection is starting, stopped or failed, Settings > WhatsApp now says so under Accounts (with Restart when it failed) instead of hiding the accounts and the Add Account button. Your links are kept; they come back once it runs.
- **Only one NotchFlow in Spotlight**: building left extra copies (Xcode's Debug and Release builds, and older backups) that showed up as separate NotchFlow apps in Spotlight and Launchpad. Builds and releases now remove the copies Xcode made once the app is installed, and keep the build and backup folders out of Spotlight. Only the three newest backups are kept (each is a whole copy of the app).
- **An AI agent no longer spins forever under the notch**: stopping Claude Code with Esc sends no "finished" event, so the notch kept showing it working ("Continuing"). NotchFlow now reads the end of the session's transcript while an agent is working and clears it when the turn was interrupted or already answered, and any agent silent for 20 minutes stops showing as working. An answer given in the notch after the agent had already moved on no longer sets it back to working.
- **"Vault not found" in Obsidian**: Orbit now checks which folders Obsidian has opened as vaults before sending a note there. If the vault folder isn't one yet, it shows the folder in Finder and says how to add it (Open folder as vault), and Settings > Orbit shows whether Obsidian has the vault open.
- **YouTube Music no longer opens tabs**: songs song.link has no YouTube Music link for are now matched by their YouTube video (the same id plays on YouTube Music), and a song that can't be matched at all opens YouTube Music's search in the same player window, brought forward to pick, instead of a new tab. If macOS blocks NotchFlow from controlling your browser, the search says so (System Settings > Privacy & Security > Automation) instead of opening a tab.
- **The music search closes with the notch**: clicking anywhere outside the notch closes the search and the notch, and the notch closing for any reason closes the search. It only keeps the notch open while you're typing in it.

## [1.2.6] - 2026-11-21

### Added
- **Glance real windows show properly, and any window can be added**: the board now leaves a clear gap where each real window sits, so you see and click the window itself (Messages, Spotify…) even when macOS won't bring that app forward. A window that can't shrink to its panel (a minimum size, a fixed mini player) is centred on it and shown whole. Floating windows like Spotify's mini player are listed too. While arranging, **Pick Window** lets you click any window on screen to put it on the board, and you can drop an app from Finder, a PDF or HTML file, or a link straight onto the board.
- **Glance uses your own browser and real windows**:
  - **Web pages** now open in your browser, signed in as you. In Chrome Beta, Chrome, Edge, Brave and Vivaldi they're borderless windows with just the page; in Safari they're normal Safari windows. They move onto the board when it opens and wait just off screen in between, so they're instant next time. Choose the browser, or NotchFlow's own pages, in Settings > Glance > Open web pages in.
  - **App panels** use the app's real window, moved onto the board and back when Glance closes. No screen recording is needed (live pictures are now an option). A window on another desktop says so and tells you how to keep that app on all desktops.
  - **Click to use**: clicking a panel or a window on the board keeps it open so you can use it; let go of the key and it stays. Tap the key or press Esc to close. Hover shows the name, and ↗ opens it in its app.
  - **Adding is smoother**: Add and Edit Layout are always in the bar at the top. The launcher searches your open browser tabs (pick one to add it, signed in) as well as popular sites, apps (their real windows, no permission prompt) and widgets. A new panel lands where your pointer was, ready to drag and resize.
- **Several terminals in the notch, and a ping when a command finishes**: the Terminal tab (Settings > Tabs & Features > Terminal) now has tabs: + opens another terminal, each keeps running on its own, × closes one. When a command that ran 10 seconds or more (you choose) finishes while you're looking elsewhere, the notch tells you ("npm finished · Terminal 2 · after 2m 13s") and that tab gets a green dot; a spinner shows on tabs with something running. Editors, ssh and agents you quit yourself don't count. The arrow opens the folder you're in, in your terminal app: iTerm2, Terminal, Ghostty, Warp, WezTerm, Alacritty, kitty or Hyper (Settings > Terminal > Open in).
- **Glance, closer to Peek**:
  - **Opening it**: the default is now Hold to peek, tap to keep open. Record Key (Settings > Glance) takes any single key (Left Shift, fn, right Command…) or a shortcut.
  - **Arrange on the board itself**: Edit (or right-click > Edit Layout) lets you drag panels anywhere on a finer grid, see where they'll land, drop one on another to swap, resize from any edge or corner, take one off with ×, and pick a ready-made arrangement. Settings uses the same editor.
  - **Add on the board**: + Add opens a launcher with Web (an address or popular sites), Widgets, Apps (every open app with its icon, then its windows as pictures; a clear Screen Recording prompt) and Files (PDF or HTML).
  - **Use app windows in place**: clicking an App window panel moves the real window into the panel so you can use it there (Spotify's buttons, a chat); it goes back when Glance closes.
  - **New panels and views**: Weather (any city, from Open-Meteo), a Week view for Calendar, Today / Scheduled / Overdue views for Reminders, HTML widgets, and agent widgets (any agent can write an HTML file into the Glance Widgets folder; it updates when the file changes).
  - **Web pages**: Back, Forward, Reload and Open in browser on hover; Hide Part of the Page (click the part, Esc cancels); trackers blocked as well as ads.
  - **More**: right-click any panel for Open in App, Reload, Edit Layout or Remove; "Same layout on all displays"; the board springs in when it opens.
- **Glance** (Settings > Glance): hold a key and your panels appear over everything as glass cards, full-screen apps included; let go and they're gone. It opens with a shortcut (⌃⌥Space) or a key on its own: fn, Shift, or the right Command, Option, Shift or Control key (typing a capital never opens it). Choose Hold (show while held, point and click panels while holding) or Toggle (press to show, press again or Esc to hide), and a clear, dimmed or blurred screen behind the panels. Panels show the real thing, edge to edge: any web page (kept loaded so it appears at once, reloads on a schedule, its own sign-ins, ads and cookie banners hidden, optional phone layout), PDFs (open on the page you left), today's calendar as a timeline with a line at now, reminders grouped by Today, Tomorrow and This week, ticked off right there, large world clocks, what's playing (artwork, progress, play, skip, shuffle, repeat), promises, window setups (click to open), the window stash (click to bring back) and WhatsApp chats. Switch panels on and off, drag them around the board in Settings (drop one on another to swap), drag a corner to resize, start from ready-made arrangements (Focus, Dashboard, Two halves, Wide on top, Sidebar), and keep several layouts to switch between, on the board too. Each display can have its own layout (Settings > Glance > Displays): when Glance opens, every display with a layout shows it. A Stocks panel shows prices, today's change and a small chart (Yahoo Finance symbols: AAPL, RELIANCE.NS, ^NSEI, BTC-USD), refreshed every minute while the board is open. App window panels show another app's window live (with Screen Recording allowed), only while the board is open; click one to go to that window.

## [1.2.4] - 2026-11-20

### Added
- **A second WhatsApp account** (Settings > WhatsApp > Add Account): link another WhatsApp next to the first, say personal next to business, by scanning its QR code. Its messages arrive in the notch the moment they come in, you can always reply from the notch (no WhatsApp app opening, no waiting for the banner), and promises and follow-ups work the same. With both linked, name them (Business, Personal) and each chat and notification shows which account it's on. Each account takes one of its phone's linked-device slots, like WhatsApp Web.
- **Connect Spotify for exact search** (Settings > Music > Spotify search): "Log in with Spotify" with your own free Spotify developer app (paste its Client ID, click Connect, agree in the browser; no password or secret is stored, the sign-in token stays in the Keychain). Then the Music tab's Spotify searches use Spotify's own catalog and play the exact track in the Spotify app, in the background. Without it, songs are still matched through song.link. When the track is found but macOS blocks NotchFlow from controlling Spotify, the notch now says so (Privacy & Security > Automation) instead of "couldn't find the exact track".
- **Search and play songs from the notch**: a search button in the Music tab lists songs as you type, with artwork, from Apple's song catalog. Pick one and it plays without leaving the notch: on Spotify by its exact track (found through song.link), in the background; from your Apple Music library right away; on YouTube Music in a browser tab opened behind what you're doing. Only when that can't be done (a song not in your Apple Music library, a track Spotify doesn't have) does the player open, also in the background. It starts with the player you use; Escape or the X closes it.
- **Artwork glow**: a soft glow of the album art's colors behind the artwork in the Music tab, brighter while playing (Settings > Music > Artwork glow).

### Changed

### Fixed
- **Window setups start apps you had quit**: opening a setup after quitting Chrome (or any app in it) now starts it again and puts its windows back, instead of waiting until you opened it yourself. The setup now follows the opening for up to half a minute: an app that didn't start is asked again, a browser that starts without its windows gets them opened (or reopened, when NotchFlow isn't allowed to control it), and each window is placed the moment it appears, however long the app takes.
- **Spotify plays without coming to the front**: with Spotify connected, a song picked in the notch is started through Spotify itself, on the device already playing or this Mac's Spotify app, so Spotify's window never moves (no more flash). When Spotify isn't open anywhere, it opens normally once and then plays. Already connected? Click Reconnect in Settings > Music and Agree once, to allow starting songs.
- **Hand cursor on search results**: hovering a song in the Music tab's search shows a pointing hand instead of the text cursor.
- **Spotify search "Insufficient client scope"**: Spotify now refuses a search that asks for "the account's country" without permission to read it. Searches send your Mac's country instead, and connecting asks for that permission (user-read-private); a connection made without it asks you to click Connect Spotify once more.
- **No more doubled notifications**: with banners read the instant they appear, a message's banner often reached the inbox before Notification Center's record of it, and the two showed as separate notifications (one with Reply, one without). The record now merges into the banner's entry: one notification, with Reply, announced once.
- **Notifications arrive instantly**: instead of checking every few seconds (up to ten on battery), NotchFlow now reacts the moment Notification Center's store changes and the moment a banner appears on screen; the timed checks remain as a slow backup.
- **Cleared notifications stay cleared**: replying to a WhatsApp message (or clearing or dismissing notifications) while its macOS banner was still on screen let the banner bring it straight back into the inbox. What you clear is remembered for a day.
- **Setups respect desktops**: a setup works on the desktop you're on. It saves and moves only that desktop's windows, never touches a window on another desktop, opens a new browser window here when the setup's is elsewhere, and no longer activates an app at the end (which could jump to another desktop where its window was and then move that window). Switching desktops while a setup is being placed stops it.

## [1.2.2] - 2026-11-20

### Added
- **Music first** (Settings > Music, on): while music plays, the closed notch shows its live activity. AI agents working, builds running, a meeting countdown and the distraction meter wait until it stops; approvals, calls and new messages still show for a moment.
- **Replying clears the conversation**: answer Rahul once and all his notifications leave the inbox (with Undo when you replied from the notch), not just the one you answered. It also happens when you reply somewhere else: in Messages on the Mac or your iPhone (seen through the Messages history, with Full Disk Access) or in WhatsApp on the linked account. Other people's messages stay. Settings > Notifications > Clear a conversation when I reply.

### Changed
- **Notifications under the notch**: a new notification shows the app's icon beside the notch and the sender and message on one line under it, like WhatsApp messages, instead of squeezing the text beside the camera where it was cut off.
- **Setups are your windows, nothing else**: Save current keeps only the windows on screen (each one's app, position, size and screen), not apps without a window, minimized windows or browser tabs, so opening a setup never opens duplicate tabs again. Opening one puts each window back exactly; an app that's closed is opened, and a window that's closed in a running app (Chrome with no window) is opened again before it's placed. Existing setups saved from the screen drop their saved pages and windowless apps once. Flow now shows what a setup did ("Updated Setup 1 to the current layout", "No windows on screen to save").

### Fixed
- **The notch closes to exactly the camera notch**: closing could end smaller than the real notch, so the camera notch showed around NotchFlow's like a second notch. The closed size is now measured from the gap between the menu bar areas beside the camera notch, a custom width or height never makes it smaller than the real notch, and moving to another screen updates the closed size too.
- **Windows stay where you put them after opening a setup**: a setup placed its windows several times over ten seconds (for apps slow to open), and every pass moved all of them back and un-minimized them, so minimizing or moving a window right after was undone. Now each window is placed once; later passes only place windows that hadn't appeared yet and stop when all are in place.
- **Replies send instantly, smart replies swipe properly, and WhatsApp numbers are right**: pressing Send closes the reply box at once and sends in the background ("Sending…" shows next to Open; a failed send reopens the box with your text), and the conversation clears after the box has closed, so the two no longer overlap. A two-finger swipe over smart reply suggestions scrolls them instead of changing the tab, with more room above and around them. WhatsApp's private chat ids ("…@lid") were taken for phone numbers, so replying opened WhatsApp at a number that doesn't exist; now only real numbers are used, otherwise the sender's number from Contacts.
- **No more repeating FaceTime alerts or stacked notifications**: during a FaceTime (or WhatsApp) call, the call-in-progress card was read as a new notification every few seconds, so it was announced again and again, alerts overlapped and the last one never went away. Calls in progress are left to the call UI, a banner seen again under a new identity is recognized as the same notification, and the same message is announced at most once in ten minutes.
- **Plans in Hinglish, Gujarati and Marathi**: "Bhai kal 5 baje coffee ke liye milte hai" answered with "Done bhai, kal 5 baje coffee ke liye ready." now becomes "Coffee with Rahul · tomorrow 5:00 PM" without the AI. Times like "kal 5 baje", "aaje saanje 7 vage", "udya sakali 9 vajta" and "kal 3pm" are understood; a yes can go on to repeat the plan ("Perfect, kal 3pm pe milte hain") while "ok but not Friday", "dekhta hu" or questions aren't a yes; the time named in the reply wins; and agreeing on a new time moves the existing plan instead of adding a second one.
- **Opened from the disk image or Downloads? NotchFlow offers to move itself**: run straight from the DMG window or a download, NotchFlow wasn't in Applications and macOS couldn't reopen it after "Quit & Reopen" (when granting Screen Recording, for example). Now it asks once at launch, copies itself into Applications (your own Applications folder on accounts that can't write to /Applications), clears the download flag, reopens from there and ejects the disk image. The installer also says where it put the app when it isn't /Applications.

## [1.2.1] - 2026-11-20

### Added
- **Window stash** (Flow tab): drag any window by its title bar up past the top of the screen into the notch and let go to park it. As the window gets close, the closed notch shows "Stash · drop here" and lights up "Let go to stash" on the spot (the trackpad taps too), and it stays closed under the dragged window: arriving at the notch with a mouse button held (dragging a window or a file) never grows, peeks or opens it by hover, so the label stays visible. Previews need Screen Recording (app icons until then); stashing needs Accessibility. Settings > Productivity > Window stash.
- **Plans you say yes to become promises**: "let's meet for coffee on Friday 5 pm" answered with "okay bhai" (or "ok", "pakka", "haan chalo", "sure", "thik che", "ho nakki", 👍…) is kept as "Coffee with Rahul · Fri 5:00 PM" with the usual reminder, whoever proposed it. Works for replies from the notch, WhatsApp on the linked account, and Messages: with Full Disk Access, NotchFlow now also sees what you send in Messages (from the Mac or your iPhone), reading new messages from a private copy of its history on this Mac (Settings > Productivity > See what I send in Messages). With "Understand any language" on, plans in Hindi, Gujarati and Marathi are understood too, and weekdays, clock times ("5pm", "17:30") and "let's" now count as hints worth checking.
- **Choose your AI provider** (Settings > Smart Tools > AI provider): MiniMax, OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Mistral, xAI (Grok), Together AI, Ollama and LM Studio on this Mac, or any OpenAI-compatible server. Each keeps its own key in the Keychain (a check mark shows which have one), address and model, so you can switch back and forth; Test, Get a key and Reset buttons. Your MiniMax key and settings carry over. Smart replies now answer in the message's own language and script, so Hinglish stays Hinglish.

### Fixed
- **"Unable to Check For Updates" on launch**: local builds from Build.command no longer start the updater (they update with git pull); Check for Updates is shown only in release builds made with Release.command. About no longer lists the update settings twice.
- **Settings that did nothing were removed**: the custom music animation ("Coming soon") and Lottie visualizer list, colour history size, timer input style and preset-list switches, expanded shelf drag area, album-art blur (replaced by Artwork glow), and ten other stored settings no code read. - **The accent color now reaches everything**: timer icons, system indicators and HUDs used an old accent setting that could no longer be changed (stuck on blue); they now follow the accent chosen in Appearance.
- **Light mode**: a Download Alerts label was drawn in white and disappeared in light mode.
- **Notch shook when switching tabs**: SwiftUI's own requests to resize the notch window during layout are now ignored (the app owns the window size) instead of being applied a moment late, where they fought the app's resize.
- **Lyrics under the closed notch now follow the lyrics button**: with lyrics turned off in the Music tab, no lyrics show under the closed notch either.
- **Crash while the notch resized**: SwiftUI's hosting view could resize the notch window from inside the window's own layout pass (`windowDidLayout`), which invalidated the layout of a scroll view mid-pass; AppKit then threw and the app aborted. The notch window now holds any resize requested during its layout pass and applies the newest one right after it.

- **Notch closing or crashing on external displays and when reopened quickly**: windows were tracked by `NSScreen` object, which AppKit replaces on every display change (attaching, waking or rearranging a monitor), so every notch window was torn down and rebuilt, sometimes during its own layout pass. Opening no longer forces a synchronous redraw from inside the SwiftUI update, invalid frames are refused instead of raising, and closing on hover exit now waits for the opening to settle and checks that the pointer really left the panel.
